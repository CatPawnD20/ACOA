# EP-02.02 — Canonical application ledger and reapplication episodes

## Scope and acceptance

Trace: F-06.06/F-10.01–05; E-04/E-05; P-11/P-15; ADR-007/013. Record canonical
owner/company/target/campaign identity, a semantic operation key, immutable
application episodes and append-only lifecycle events. Distinct suitable positions
remain distinct; transport URL, channel, account, document and attempt changes do
not create new operation identity. No execution, submission verification, email,
browser, real profile or employer is activated.

## Identity and concurrency

The trusted canonicalization adapter supplies company and opportunity/contact
UUIDs plus a resolution-evidence reference. This ledger does not discover aliases,
normalize arbitrary URLs, verify employers or authenticate evidence by itself.
Unresolved identity must be held for review by its owner and the Policy Gate;
registration records intent, not verified identity or sending permission. Organization,
opportunity and general evidence registries are later owners, so their UUID
references do not yet have cross-registry foreign keys. Series/episode/predecessor
relationships have actual database foreign keys and unique constraints.

The semantic key follows the approved owner/company/target/campaign/kind tuple,
adding a reapplication episode only for a genuine new episode. A second initial
episode is also blocked across campaigns for the same canonical target: changing
a campaign label cannot bypass prior history. A row lock on the canonical series
serializes registration and closure. Any future executor changing application
delivery state must acquire this series lock before the application row to keep
the same concurrency order. Exact repeated registration is idempotent; conflicting
specifications cannot reuse an application UUID. App role can append/read events
but cannot update/delete them.

## Reapplication and authority

A new episode must reference the latest rejected/closed application in the same
series, use a distinct episode UUID and have exact content-bound, unexpired
evidence produced after closure. Unknown policy, missing meaningful-change proof,
unelapsed cooldown, missing channel confirmation or missing fresh authorization
all block registration. No numeric cooldown/change criterion is invented: these
remain user-configured policy inputs. Evidence records contain explicit UUID
references and confirmations; only a trusted policy adapter may assemble them.
They are not signed proof and must never be accepted from an agent/browser body.

Lifecycle and delivery state are separate. Closure cannot erase or fabricate a
submission result; unconfirmed delivery blocks closure/reapplication. Registering
an episode leaves submission `not_started`. There is no public method here that
marks a draft submitted. The later executor must resolve current authoritative
grants/consents and reservations again; an audit decision UUID or this registration
cannot authorize a send. Generic evidence authentication/ingestion and automatic
reapplication policy configuration are not claimed by this task.

runtime checks passed for exact registration idempotency, immutable identity,
