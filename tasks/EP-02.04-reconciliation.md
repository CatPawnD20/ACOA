# EP-02.04 — Evidence-backed reconciliation and user-review incidents

## Scope and acceptance

Trace: F-08/F-10; E-04.01–07; P-11/P-21; ADR-007/013/014. Resolve a dispatched
attempt only from exact, supported, trusted-verifier evidence. Missing results
remain unconfirmed and never become a second send. Persist immutable source
references/evidence hashes, reconciliation events, deduplicated incidents and a
pending user-review outbox. No provider lookup, notification delivery, automatic
retry, live employer or public evidence-ingestion endpoint is supplied here.

## Evidence and lifecycle rules

Evidence binds the exact attempt, provider and complete preparation hash. Its
observation must be after dispatch and no later than database time. The default
`verified_by_adapter=false` cannot resolve delivery. A trusted read-only verifier
must authenticate the source, fetch/retain its protected snapshot and validate
the claim before setting that field; typed values/hashes are not signatures or
proof of origin. Agents/LLMs must never supply these records directly. General
source/artifact persistence and actual Gmail/browser verification belong to their
later adapters; opaque source references are not yet cross-registry foreign keys.

Sent records, confirmation pages and authoritative status can establish provider
acceptance. Only a delivery receipt establishes delivered; only a read receipt
establishes read. These explicit claims remain separate in stored evidence.
Neither a transport acknowledgement nor a successful draft proves submission.
An empty search is inconclusive: confirmed non-performance requires definitive
authoritative status. Unknown/malformed/foreign/unverified proof cannot release
an operation lock or manufacture success/failure.

Only reconciling/unconfirmed or already terminal dispatched attempts accept
reconciliation. Prepared/reserved/executing work cannot be declared submitted by
this interface. Supported positive evidence produces verified submission;
definitive non-performance produces failed submission. Neither state returns to
a sendable state. Repeated identical evidence is idempotent and cannot change its
UUID content. Contradictory terminal evidence retains the prior result, records
both proof and an incident, and pauses the system for human review.

Unknown transport response atomically records an incident/outbox item. Repeated
unknown lookup deduplicates the open incident. Resolving unknown/abandoned work
with definitive evidence resolves those incidents and their pending outbox rows;
invalid/conflicting/cost incidents remain for review. The outbox is a durable
notification request, **not a delivered user notification**. Notification transport
and dashboard handling remain EP-10 scope.

Known matching-currency cost is recorded exactly, releasing unused reservation.
Unknown cost retains its hold even after delivery is verified. Currency mismatch
retains the hold without conversion. Proven cost above the reservation, including
when the outcome remains unknown, opens a cost incident and pauses the system.
Actual overruns are recorded honestly rather than clamped to their estimate.
Consumed cost is accounted on reconciliation date in the configured timezone;
unsettled holds remain charged across periods. Conflicting later cost evidence
cannot silently overwrite an earlier settlement.

Abandoned recovery uses the committed dispatch deadline, locks current state and
marks expired executing work unconfirmed with a review incident. It never invokes
a provider, resends or assumes a crashed worker did not transmit. This also covers
a crash after committed dispatch but before actual provider invocation. A real
process-kill exercise is EP-02.05; this task exercised elapsed-deadline recovery.

## Verification — 2026-10-15

Applied `0004_reconciliation` on dedicated local ACOA PostgreSQL. Direct runtime
probes passed: unknown incident/outbox deduplication, retained unknown cost hold,
wrong attempt, false adapter verification, unsupported read/non-performance claim,
exact accepted proof, duplicate immutable proof, evidence-ID tamper rejection,
separate read receipt, definitive failure, expired executing recovery and no
second execution token. Verified application state and exact consumed/released
money. Contradictory evidence and reservation overrun paused the system; the prior
terminal result remained intact. Evidence/event update/delete were denied.

A focused additional probe confirmed that a proven overrun with unknown outcome
pauses without releasing its hold; later accepted evidence settles the exact
actual cost while preserving one cost incident. All probe changes, including
synthetic permissions, incidents and pause, were rolled back. No real provider,
personal data, test/fixture files or external notification was involved.

All 98 existing tests, Ruff lint/format, strict mypy (32 source/check files and
seven deployment/migration modules) and diff checks passed. The final incident
guard rejected unrelated/missing evidence without mutation. Rebuilt the Linux
maintenance image; repeated upgrade/current reported `0004_reconciliation`.
Hosted CI, real source authenticity/retention, actual
notification delivery and process-kill verification are not claimed here.
