# EP-10.06 — Explicit limited production activation

## Scope and requirement links

This task owns the final production activation boundary for `F-06`, `F-07`, `F-08`,
`F-09`, `E-04`, `E-07`, `E-08`, `E-11`, `P-01`, `P-02`, `P-04`, `P-05`, `P-08`,
`P-11`, `P-17`, `P-21`, `ADR-006`, `ADR-007`, `ADR-011` and `ADR-013`.

The user approved a private, finite, limited-email V1 scope. The approval covers only
first applications and first outreach to verified official application addresses or
verified public corporate recruiting contacts. It permits only the approved professional
profile and application-material categories. Sensitive data remains outside scope. Employer
replies and follow-up messages remain draft-only and user-sent.

## Required activation boundary

- Represent the approved recipient class as a content-bound, versioned authority record.
  A discovered address is not authorized merely because it matches an email pattern.
- Enrol each concrete recipient only after company identity, professional purpose, public
  corporate provenance, channel freshness and opt-out status have trusted evidence.
- Bind each action to an enrolled recipient, current grant, exact material versions, data
  categories, quota, budget and duplicate checks through the central Policy Gate.
- Persist the user's exact activation review, finite validity window and conservative ramp
  limits. Scope expansion or renewal requires a new review.
- Activate atomically only when current readiness, verified pilot evidence, zero open
  incidents, approved rendered application material and a configured production discovery
  source all match the current Policy Authority revision.
- Retain the existing `prepare -> reserve -> execute once -> reconcile` protocol. An
  ambiguous result pauses the application and cannot be retried blindly.
- Safe mode, user pause, expiry, revocation or systemic risk must immediately restore
  `dry_run=true` and `external_actions_enabled=false` without erasing evidence.

## Current gate

The private scope approval is recorded, but production remains blocked. Dynamic recipient
classes and immutable per-target enrollment are implemented, including current grant,
opportunity, company verification, public corporate channel, freshness and opt-out checks at
both reservation and execution. The approved production grant and concrete recipients have not
yet been enrolled. The finite Arbeitnow public-API policy and successful bounded live
discovery/fetch provenance are recorded locally. The approved general CV baseline passed a
one-page local render review and opened read-only in Microsoft Word. That baseline is not a
submission artifact: every target requires a separately composed, immutable and visually checked
application-specific CV bound to the exact opportunity and verified profile facts. Until a target,
company, channel and concrete recipient are verified and enrolled, the Policy Authority must
remain in safe mode and no employer action may execute.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Unverified, private, guessed, stale, opted-out or out-of-scope recipients fail before
   provider I/O.
2. Reply and follow-up send attempts remain impossible even after activation.
3. A current enrolled recipient and exact approved material can pass every deterministic
   gate within the private ramp limits.
4. Stale readiness, expired review, open incident, material mismatch or missing discovery
   evidence blocks activation atomically.
5. Activation and safe-mode rollback are both auditable, idempotent and restart-safe.
6. No secret, address, CV content or private approval payload enters Git.
