# EP-01.03 — Deterministic Policy Gate

## Scope and acceptance

Implement pure ordered preflight with `ALLOW`, `BLOCK`, `NEED_USER`, and `RECHECK`.
Trace: EP-01.03; E-07.01/02/04–07; P-01/P-02/P-03/P-04/P-06/P-10/P-11/P-17;
ADR-006/007. No provider, execution token, reservation, database access or real
external action is implemented by this gate.

- Preserve safe defaults; pause suppresses work. Fixed action permission cannot
  be overridden by a grant. Authoritative reply/follow-up purpose also blocks a
  relabeled initial application or document upload.
- Require current, exact grant/action/channel/account/recipient/data scopes and
  separate sensitive consent. Bind trusted evidence to the exact request hash;
  bind material classification/integrity to its exact content hash/categories.
- Check hard conditions, company/channel/account trust, corporate outreach,
  opt-out, duplicate/ambiguous delivery, declared quotas, projected budget,
  current artifacts/profile, personal answers, access challenges and nonbinding
  terms in named stages. No numeric match score can bypass them.
- Unknown usage/evidence never becomes zero or satisfied. Budget arithmetic uses
  finite Decimal values and matching currency labels without conversion.

## Trust and implementation limits

`PolicyContext` must be assembled by trusted adapters from authoritative state,
not deserialized from an agent plan or browser request. Typed evidence and hashes
are not authenticated proof by themselves. Context usage must correspond to the
exact selected recipient/account/action and policy reference. A preflight ALLOW
is not a sending capability: the future executor must atomically recheck current
grant/consent versions, reserve quota/budget and re-evaluate before executing once.

Quota snapshots describe usage before this initial-action reservation; future
execution rechecks must account for their own reservation instead of charging it
twice. Career-account creation is not counted as an initial application, while
its current limit-policy identity and monetary/token/time boundaries remain checked.
Profile classification and recipient verification are required adapter outputs;
the gate does not classify PII or establish account ownership itself.

Research is permitted in dry-run only when source access/challenge evidence is
satisfied and retrieval cost is known free or within budget. Draft-only actions
do not send. Generated tools may be allowed only in verified sandbox mode; no
sandbox runner is supplied here. Runtime code/production modification remains deny;
that restriction governs ACOA, not separately authorized human engineering work.
