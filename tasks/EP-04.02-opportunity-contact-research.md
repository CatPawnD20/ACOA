# EP-04.02 — Opportunity and corporate contact research

Trace: F-02.03; F-03.01–07; F-04.01/F-04.03–07; E-05/E-06/E-07;
P-08/P-16; ADR-002/005/006/013.

## Scope and behavior

Organization identity is derived from one canonical verified domain and backed by an
unexpired source snapshot reference. Official URLs, email domains and channel hosts are
separate explicit evidence scopes. Different paths on the same verified domain therefore
retain one organization identity without treating a similar company name as proof.

Opportunities use explicit `published_job` and `unadvertised_prospect` kinds. Published
jobs require sourced employer, role and canonical verified application URL fields.
Unadvertised prospects remain `unverified` and cannot claim a listing URL or deadline.
Every present normalized field carries its own evidence and confidence; absent values
remain `missing` rather than being inferred. Stable opportunity identity is separate from
immutable version content, so status or sourced-field changes preserve prior versions.

Contact research distinguishes career portals, general-application email, public
corporate email, career forms and support forms. A candidate must be observed in current
public source evidence. Private/personal visibility, unknown visibility, inferred email
patterns, stale evidence, malformed endpoints and email domains outside the explicitly
verified organization scope are rejected before persistence. Support and unrelated
purposes can be recorded as non-suitable context but never become eligible initial-contact
channels. An empty accepted set produces an explicit `no_channel` result; no address is
invented. These records do not authorize or send a message.

## Persistence

Revision `0011_opportunity_research` adds immutable organization records, versioned
opportunities, versioned contact channels and contact-research results. The application
role receives read/insert access only. Repository writes are exact-idempotent and reject
reuse of an organization ID, opportunity version, channel version or research ID with
different content. Channel endpoint values are retained only for verified public
professional/corporate channels; rejected private candidates are represented by
structured reason codes and are not stored.

## Observed local verification

- A transient deterministic probe built one verified organization, one published job and
  one unadvertised prospect. It rejected an active-listing claim for the prospect and
  preserved missing fields without inventing values.
- Contact research accepted a sourced recruiting email, retained a support form as
  ineligible, and rejected private-personal and inferred addresses. No network, model,
  browser, mail or employer action occurred.
- Alembic reported the single head `0011_opportunity_research`; the retained dedicated
  PostgreSQL volume migrated from `0010_source_discovery` and stayed healthy.
- An application-role transaction wrote one organization, one opportunity version, two
  channel versions and one research result. Exact repeats were idempotent, a conflicting
  opportunity version was rejected, direct update was denied, and all synthetic rows were
  rolled back. The temporary loopback port was removed afterward.
- Existing tests, lint, formatting, strict type checks, migration graph/offline SQL and
  package consistency were run without adding or changing test files or utilities.

## Remaining boundaries

Trusted parsing from real source bytes is not implemented, and structural evidence
objects are not proof of authenticated ingestion by themselves. Organization aliases,
cross-source opportunity matching and uncertain deduplication remain EP-04.04. Employer
risk scoring, fake-portal signals and opt-out policy remain EP-04.03. This task performs no
provider call, personal-email lookup, message preparation, submission or external write.
